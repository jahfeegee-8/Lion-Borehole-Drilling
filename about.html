Our Story

Your Water. Your Freedom.

Lion Borehole Drilling & Civils was founded with one clear purpose: to give South Africans reliable access to their own water — free from the pressures of high municipal bills, water restrictions and unpredictable supply.

We serve homes, farms, smallholdings, businesses and estates across South Africa. Whether you're in a city suburb where municipal pressure has dropped, or on a remote farm that's never had a reliable water source, we come to you.
